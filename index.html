Our Story

Cheesesteaks made with real love.

Wiz Kidz Cheesesteaks was born from a simple obsession: the perfect Philly cheesesteak. We use premium ribeye, melted provolone, and fresh-baked hoagie rolls to create a cheesesteak experience that’s bold, authentic, and absolutely unforgettable. Every bite is stacked with flavor.

Build Your Order

How To Order Like A Pro

Creating your dream Wiz Kidz cheesesteak is simple. Follow the 3 steps to flavor perfection.

STEP 01

Pick Your Vessel

Choose how you want your meal built:

  • Amoroso Roll: Fresh-baked Philly hoagie
  • Pierogie Bowl: Over fried buffalo pierogies
  • Fry Bowl: Over hand-cut seasoned fries
  • Wrap: Warm toasted flour tortilla
STEP 02

Choose Your Protein

Sizzled fresh on our flat-top grill:

  • Hand-Sliced Ribeye: Pure juicy steak
  • Seasoned Chicken: Tender chopped breast
  • Double Meat Option: For serious appetites
STEP 03

Say The Cheese

Melted to perfection over onions & peppers:

  • House Whiz: Warm creamy gold
  • Cooper Sharp: Extra rich & creamy
  • Provolone: Classic sharp stretch
  • American: Melty classic comfort
