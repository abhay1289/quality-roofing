About Us

A Cleaner Home, Done With Care.

Philly Shine Cleaning Co. helps Philadelphia homeowners enjoy a spotless, stress-free home with dependable residential cleaning and a polished, professional experience from start to finish.

Our story starts long before the business did. Growing up, our parents believed there was always something to do around the house — even on weekends. They’d wake us up, hand us a broom, and teach us that a clean home wasn’t just about appearances, it was about care. Scrubbing bathrooms and sweeping floors as kids taught us a work ethic that never left us.

  • Reliable arrival windows & clear communication
  • Checklist-based, detail-focused cleaning
  • Consistent results you can count on
  • Flexible add-ons when you need extra attention
